Click or tap the map to add vertices (maximum 50). Use Undo to remove the last point or Reset to clear the draft. Apply requires at least 3 vertices; the polygon closes automatically.

To clear an applied polygon, Reset and then Apply with no vertices. Changes are not sent until Apply.

Orange squares: supplied 0.5° detection cells. Map: 110–155° E, 45–9° S; offshore territories outside this area are not shown.
